VibeCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

VibeCheck helps you notice how your mood relates to the things you take. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does with it.

The short version

  • Your data stays on your devices. VibeCheck has no accounts, no servers of its own and no analytics. We don't receive, collect or store your data anywhere.
  • iCloud sync is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, your check-ins and substances are stored in your own private iCloud so they appear on your other devices. Apple Health numbers are never synced.
  • We don't sell or share your data, or use it for advertising or tracking.
  • You are in control. You can export your check-ins, delete them, and revoke Apple Health access at any time.

What the app stores on your device

  • Check-ins: your mood (good or bad), the time, which substances you tapped and, if you set it, how many, and what prompted the check-in (you, a reminder, or a heart-rate prompt).
  • Your substance list, including any you add yourself.
  • Your settings: reminder times, quiet hours, daily limit, snooze.
  • Apple Health summaries, if you connect Health: an average heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, last night's sleep, your steps over the previous 24 hours, and the minutes of workouts in those 24 hours, saved with each check-in. When you connect Health, or after an update that starts reading steps, VibeCheck also fills these in for your earlier check-ins from Health's own history.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) only if you have device backups turned on, under your own Apple account settings. Unless you turn on iCloud sync (below), VibeCheck itself never uploads it.

Answering without opening the app

Reminders offer Good and Bad buttons, and Shortcuts, the Action Button and a Home or Lock Screen widget can start a check-in. These only add a mood (or open the app). They never show your data, and the widget contains none. Anything that opens the app waits for Face ID or your passcode if app lock is on.

Control Center and Lock Screen controls (optional)

The Good and Bad controls can't write to VibeCheck's data themselves. A tap leaves a mood and a time in a small storage area shared only between VibeCheck and its widget, on your iPhone. VibeCheck saves it as a check-in and deletes it from that area the next time it runs. Nothing else is stored there, and none of it leaves your iPhone.

Apple Watch (optional)

If you install the Watch app, its Good and Bad buttons send a mood and a time to the VibeCheck app on your paired iPhone, directly between the two devices (Apple's WatchConnectivity). Nothing else is sent, nothing is stored on the Watch apart from the last tap, and it never goes through a server.

App lock (optional)

In Settings → Privacy you can require Face ID (or your passcode) to open VibeCheck, and choose how soon it locks again. While the app isn't in use its contents are hidden from the app switcher. The check is done by iOS. VibeCheck never sees or stores your face or passcode data. If your iPhone's passcode is later removed, the lock turns itself off rather than locking you out.

iCloud sync (optional)

In Settings → iCloud you can sync your check-ins, your substance list, and their moods between your own devices using your private iCloud (Apple's CloudKit). It's off until you turn it on.

  • What syncs: moods, times, which substances you tapped (and how many), and your substance list.
  • What never syncs: heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, steps and workouts from Apple Health. These stay on the iPhone they were recorded on, as Apple's rules require. Sample data is also not used while sync is on.
  • Who can see it: the data lives in your private iCloud database, which only your Apple account can access. Apple encrypts it, but unless you've turned on Advanced Data Protection, Apple can access it. We (the developer) cannot see it.
  • Deleting: "Delete check-ins only" and "Delete all data and reset" remove them from iCloud as well. Turning sync off stops syncing but does not delete what's already in iCloud. Delete check-ins first if you want it gone.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. VibeCheck asks only to read heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, steps and workouts. It never writes to Health. Health data is used only to add context to your check-ins, to show patterns in the Insights tab, and to decide whether to offer a check-in when your resting heart rate has been elevated. It is never used for advertising, never shared, and never stored outside your device. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app.

A heart-rate prompt is a nudge to check in. It is not a medical alert or advice.

Notifications

If you allow them, VibeCheck sends local reminders from your device. No notification content passes through any server.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings → Your data → Export check-ins (CSV). You choose where the file goes.
  • Import: Settings → Your data → Import check-ins (CSV) reads a file made by VibeCheck's own export (for example to bring your history back after getting a new iPhone). It is read on your iPhone, shows you what it will add before adding anything, skips check-ins you already have, and never uploads the file.
  • Share a summary: Settings → Your data → Share a summary (PDF) builds a short report on your iPhone (overview, how often each substance was logged, clear patterns, and a log of your check-ins, optionally with your Apple Health numbers) and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes, for example to a doctor. VibeCheck doesn't send it anywhere itself, and sample data is never included.
  • Delete: Settings → Your data → Delete check-ins only, or Delete all data and reset, which also removes the substances you added, resets the built-in ones and your reminder settings, and brings back the sample data, like a fresh install. This also removes them from iCloud if sync is on. Deleting the app removes the data on that device.

This website

This website is plain pages. It has no cookies, no analytics and no advertising or tracking scripts. Like any web host, the service that hosts it may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation.

Children

VibeCheck is not intended for children and is rated for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, and the app has no way to send us any.

Medical disclaimer

VibeCheck is for personal tracking and self-awareness. It does not provide medical advice and cannot diagnose or treat any condition. If you're worried about your health or your use of any substance, please talk to a healthcare professional. See Support for helplines.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
