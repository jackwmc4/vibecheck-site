VibeCheck support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach a person.

Contact

Email support@jackmac.net. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what happened. Don't send your check-in data unless we ask. We can usually help without it.

If you need help right now

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

If you're struggling or worried about your use of alcohol, drugs or medicines, free confidential helplines exist in most countries. findahelpline.com lists them by country. In the app, Settings → Support and helplines shows services for your country without needing an internet connection. These are independent services, not run by VibeCheck.

Using the app

How do I check in?
Tap Check in now on the Today tab, choose Good or Bad, tap anything you've had, and save. You can also tap Good or Bad on a reminder (press and hold it), use the Shortcuts action "Log a vibe", the Action Button, a Lock Screen or Control Center control, the widget, or your Apple Watch.
I forgot to log something earlier.
Open the History tab and tap ＋ to add a check-in for a time that has already passed. Tap any check-in to fix or delete it.
Why does Insights say "not enough data"?
Patterns need a fair number of check-ins both with and without a substance. Until there are enough, VibeCheck says so instead of guessing. Sample data shown on a fresh install is only there so you can see how the screens work. It's never used for your insights.
Can I change how many reminders I get, or when?
Yes. Settings has reminder times, quiet hours and a daily limit, and any reminder can be snoozed until tomorrow. Settings → How timing works explains how reminders adapt.

Reminders aren't showing up

  • In the iPhone's Settings → Notifications → VibeCheck, make sure notifications are allowed.
  • Check that no Focus mode or Do Not Disturb is hiding them, and that the time isn't inside your quiet hours.
  • Reminders adapt to when you tend to check in, so there may be a short learning period.

Apple Health

What does VibeCheck read from Health?
Heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, steps and workouts. It only reads them. It never writes to Health. Health is optional.
I tapped "Don't allow" by mistake.
Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps → VibeCheck, and turn on the categories you want. You can also revoke access there at any time.
What's the heart-rate prompt?
If your resting heart rate has been elevated for a while, VibeCheck can offer a check-in. It's a nudge, not a medical alert or advice.

Your data

Where is my data?
On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, check-ins and substances are also in your private iCloud, so they appear on your other devices. Health numbers never sync.
How do I turn on iCloud sync?
Sign in to iCloud on the iPhone, then go to Settings → iCloud in VibeCheck and turn on Sync. Close and reopen the app. Sync takes effect when the app opens.
How do I move to a new iPhone?
Either turn on iCloud sync on both phones, or use Settings → Your data → Export check-ins (CSV) on the old one and Import check-ins (CSV) on the new one. Importing shows what it will add first and skips check-ins you already have.
How do I send a summary to my doctor?
Settings → Your data → Share a summary (PDF) builds a short report on your iPhone. You choose where it goes. It never includes sample data.
How do I delete my data?
Settings → Your data has Delete check-ins only and Delete all data and reset, which also removes substances you added. If sync is on, this removes them from iCloud too. Deleting the app removes the data on that device.

Apple Watch and controls

I tapped Good on my Watch. Where is it?
The Watch sends it straight to your iPhone. If the phone is out of range, it's delivered when they reconnect, and the Watch shows its checkmark only once the iPhone has confirmed it.
The Good and Bad controls in Control Center don't save instantly.
A control can't write to the app's data itself. It leaves a note that VibeCheck saves the next time it opens or comes to the foreground, stamped with the time you tapped. It appears in History when you next open the app.

App lock

Can I lock the app?
Yes. Settings → Privacy lets you require Face ID or your passcode, and choose how soon it locks again. If you later remove your iPhone passcode, the lock turns itself off rather than locking you out.
